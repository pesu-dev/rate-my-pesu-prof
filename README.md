# Rate My Prof – PES Edition

A transparent, anonymous, and student-first professor review platform built specifically for PES University (RR & EC Campuses).

Find honest insights from fellow students, rate your professors across multiple academic criteria, and make informed course choices.

---

## Key Features

- Search & Discover: Filter professors by department, campus (RR / EC), and minimum star ratings.
- Verified Student Login: Secure Single Sign-On (SSO) via PESU Academy credentials to prevent spam while keeping your identity 100% anonymous.
- Detailed Rating Criteria: Rate professors on overall quality, teaching effectiveness, course ease, grading strictness, and attendance policy.
- Custom Student Tags: Highlight key traits like Tough Grader, Inspiring, Slides Reader, or Attendance Mandatory.
- AI-Powered Sentiment Analysis: Reviews are automatically analyzed by LLMs to display overall sentiment trends (Positive / Neutral / Negative).
- Automated Content Moderation: Strict profanity filtering and anti-abuse systems to ensure constructive, respectful, and genuine academic feedback.
- Request Missing Professors: Can't find a professor? Submit a request to add them directly to the directory.

---

## How to Use the Platform

### 1. Browse & Search Professors
- Visit the home catalog to browse all professors across RR Campus and EC Campus.
- Use the search bar to find professors by name.
- Filter by department or minimum rating (e.g., 4+ Stars).

### 2. View Detailed Professor Profiles
- Click on any professor's card to view their complete breakdown.
- See rating distributions, average teaching quality, course difficulty, student tags, and written reviews.

### 3. Sign In to Submit a Review
- Click Sign In to Review and log in with your PESU Academy SRN and Password.
- Your credentials are used only once to confirm you are a current PES University student.
- Your name and SRN are never stored with your review.

### 4. Rate & Share Feedback
- Rate overall quality and specific categories (Teaching, Ease, Grading, Attendance) from 1 to 5 stars.
- Select relevant student tags.
- Write a detailed, constructive review describing your experience in the class.

## Anonymity & Privacy Guarantees

We take student privacy extremely seriously:

- No Passwords Stored: We verify logins directly via the PESU-Auth API. Your password is never saved, logged, or accessible to system administrators.
- Cryptographic Hashing: Your SRN is converted into a one-way cryptographic hash. This ensures each student can only review a professor once without storing who wrote the review.
- Complete Anonymity: Every review is published under the tag Anonymous Student.

---

## Community Guidelines & Moderation

To keep the platform helpful for all PES students, all reviews must follow these rules:

1. Be Constructive & Specific: Focus on teaching style, exam style, workload, and course structure.
2. No Harassment or Profanity: Severe profanity, personal insults, slurs, or hate speech are automatically rejected by our multi-stage moderation filter.
3. Trust Score System: Submitting clean reviews increases your account trust score. Attempting to post abusive content or profanity reduces your score and will result in an automatic shadow ban.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

- Is my review really anonymous?
  Yes. Your PESU login is strictly used to prove you are a PES student. Your SRN is hashed immediately and cannot be traced back to your review text.

- Can professors remove bad reviews?
  No. Reviews are only moderated or removed if they violate community rules (profanity, personal attacks, or spam).

- What if my professor isn't listed?
  Click "Report a Missing Professor" at the bottom of the home page to submit their name, campus, and department for administrative review.
